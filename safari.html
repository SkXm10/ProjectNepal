Untamed Wildlife Safaris

Journey into subtropical jungles and encounter rare exotic species

Top Rated

Chitwan National Park

Nepal's premier wildlife haven. Embark on jeep safaris to track rare one-horned rhinoceroses, wild elephants, crocodiles, and the elusive Bengal tiger through dense sal forests.

Tiger Haven

Bardia National Park

The largest undisturbed wilderness in Nepal's western plains. Known for offering the absolute best chances for tracking wild Royal Bengal tigers during pristine, quiet walking safaris.

Bird Watching

Koshi Tappu Reserve

A paradise for bird lovers located on the vast mudflats of the Koshi River. It harbors over 500 species of migratory birds and the last remaining herd of wild water buffaloes in Nepal.

Grasslands

Shuklaphanta National Park

Famous for its massive, open golden grasslands. It protects the world's largest single herd of majestic swamp deer (Barasingha), alongside herds of wild elephants and leopards.
